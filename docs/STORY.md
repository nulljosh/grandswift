# Story

Three people. One rainy city. Rent is four grand and nobody has it.

**Joshua** is the downtown hustler. Fast talker, bad ideas, good at running.
**Ben** cruises Kits. Chill, connected, knows every dealer and every dad with a boat.
**Alexandre** works an office job in Victoria and does deliveries on the side. The only one with a plan.

You switch between them any time. Their stories cross at the heist.

## Act one: Joshua gets in over his head

**1. Out the door.** The game starts mid-run. Joshua bursts out of the Apple Store on Georgia with a bag of Mac minis. Two employees in blue shirts chase him onto the sidewalk. One is on the phone to the cops. *Get out of here.*

**2. Jack a car.** One star, a siren getting closer. Grab the first car at the curb.

**3. Lose the cops.** Stars climb when you hit things and drain when you break line of sight. Lose them past the Art Gallery.

**4. Pawn the Mac minis.** There's no inventory screen. A line in the corner says *Mac minis: 6*. Sell them on Granville. The pawn guy pays half what you hoped and asks where you got them.

**5. The debt.** The minis were never Joshua's idea. They were a payment. He owes Dmitri, a crypto guy in a Patagonia vest who runs a fake co-working space in Gastown. Dmitri wants forty grand by Friday.

## Act two: Ben and Alexandre

**6. Kits Beach.** Switch to Ben. Joshua calls from a payphone, which Ben finds hilarious. Ben needs a boat. He borrows one from a dad at the Kits yacht club, sort of.

**7. The Lululemon sale.** A stampede on 4th Avenue. Ben uses the chaos to lift a delivery van full of sample stock. It's worth nothing. The van is the prize.

**8. The ferry.** Switch to Alexandre in Victoria. He drives the van onto the Swartz Bay ferry, finds out the truck parked next to him belongs to Dmitri, and follows it home.

**9. Government Street.** Alexandre learns what Dmitri actually does: he launders money through condo presales. The money moves on Saturday from a sales centre in Coal Harbour.

## Act three: the heist

**10. The plan.** All three in one room for the first time, Ben's dad's garage. Pick the approach:
- **Loud:** hit the armoured truck on the Lions Gate Bridge.
- **Quiet:** crash the presale party dressed as realtors and walk the cash out the front.

**11. Crew and gear.** Pick a driver and a hacker from people you've met. Cheaper crew take a bigger cut of nothing when they mess up.

**12. The score.** Pull the job. Each hero handles one part and you switch between them mid-heist.

**13. The getaway.** Every cop in the city. The boat from mission 6 is the way out, under the bridge and across to North Van.

**14. Waterfront.** Pay Dmitri, or don't. Two endings. Either way, Vancouver is yours and the game opens up.

## After the story

Open world. Side jobs and random events all over the city: bike lane rage, seagulls stealing your hot dog at English Bay, SkyTrain surfing, a film crew blocking the street (steal the prop cop car), Tesla drag races in Coal Harbour, a 2011-style hockey riot. Tab flies you to Victoria, Seattle, Toronto or New York, streamed from the real city.

## What exists today (Unreal build)
Missions 1 to 4 run as walk-to checkpoints at the real Apple Store, Art Gallery, Granville and Waterfront. The sports car on Georgia takes E to get in and out. Fast travel and cops are written and waiting to go in. Everything past mission 4 is script.
