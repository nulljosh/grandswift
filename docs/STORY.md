# Story: the opener

The game starts mid-run. No menu, no cutscene. You're Joshua, bursting out of the Apple Store on Georgia with a bag of Mac minis, and two employees in blue shirts are right behind you. One of them is already on the phone to the cops.

## 1. Out the door
You spawn at the store's front doors, running. The employees chase you onto the sidewalk. They can't really stop you, but they slow you down if they catch you. The screen says: *Get out of here.*

## 2. Jack a car
By the time you hit the curb you've got one star and a siren getting closer. Grab the first car on Georgia. The street gets busy fast, so you don't have long.

## 3. Lose the cops
Drive. Stars climb if you hit things and drop if you break line of sight. Lose them past the Art Gallery and the heat fades.

## 4. Pawn the Mac minis
There's no inventory screen, it's not an RPG. A small line in the corner just says what you're carrying: *Mac minis: 6*. Take them to a pawn shop on Granville, sell them, the line clears, and the cash goes up.

## 5. Vancouver is yours
Mission complete. From here it's open world: steal cars, rob stores, cause trouble, switch to Ben or Alexandre, or just drive around the real city.

## What exists today
The mission chain runs in Unreal as walk-to checkpoints at these real spots: the Apple Store, the Art Gallery, Granville and Waterfront Station. Still to build: employees chasing you, stealing cars and cops in the Unreal build (the browser build already has both), the carrying line and a pawn shop.
