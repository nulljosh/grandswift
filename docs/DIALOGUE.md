# Dialogue: Missions 6-14

Spoken lines for Act Two and Act Three. One-line trigger condition per mission.

---

## Mission 6: Kits Beach

**Objective:** Get Dad's boat for the job.

**Dialogue:**
> **Joshua** (on payphone): "Ben, I need the boat."
> **Ben:** "Ask Dad yourself."
> **Joshua:** "You know he'll just ask about the hard drive again."
> **Brian** (arriving): "What hard drive? The one you borrowed two years ago?"

**Trigger:** Fires when player enters radius near Kits Beach dock (Ben's current location).

---

## Mission 7: The Lululemon Sale

**Objective:** Grab the delivery van in the stampede.

**Dialogue:**
> **Ben** (watching crowds): "This is peak Vancouver. Rich people fighting over yoga pants."
> **Crowd member:** "That's the new Essential!"
> **Ben:** (grabbing keys from unattended dock): "Enjoy the search."

**Trigger:** Fires when player collects keys from van during 4th Avenue stampede radius.

---

## Mission 8: The Ferry

**Objective:** Follow Dmitri's truck off the Swartz Bay ferry.

**Dialogue:**
> **Alexandre** (in van, docked): "Okay, that's... Dmitri's truck. Right there."
> **Ferry worker:** "Keep your lane, buddy."
> **Alexandre:** "Yeah. Definitely keeping my lane."

**Trigger:** Fires when player's van enters ferry departure checkpoint near Sidney.

---

## Mission 9: Government Street

**Objective:** Scope the presale centre and confirm the cash pickup Saturday.

**Dialogue:**
> **Alexandre** (outside luxury tower): "Coal Harbour. Saturday. Money moves through here."
> **Real estate agent** (walking past): "Are you interested in a two-bed?"
> **Alexandre:** "Not even a little bit."

**Trigger:** Fires when player enters Government Street presale centre radius.

---

## Mission 10: The Plan

**Objective:** Gather the crew and pick your approach.

**Dialogue:**
> **Joshua:** "Quiet or loud?"
> **Ben:** "Loud is faster."
> **Alexandre:** "Quiet works if nobody panics."
> **Brian** (setting down sandwiches): "Your mother made these. Don't get them bloody."
> **Christine** (from kitchen): "I didn't hear any of that!"

**Trigger:** Fires when all three players enter Dad's garage radius.

---

## Mission 11: Crew and Gear

**Objective:** Convince Sarah to be the hacker.

**Dialogue:**
> **Joshua:** "We need you."
> **Sarah:** "Get a different hacker."
> **Joshua:** "There isn't one better than you."
> **Sarah:** "That's the problem. You owe me a favour."
> **Joshua:** "Name it."
> **Sarah:** "Drive me to an interview in Burnaby. On time. No cops, no running red lights, no stealing cars."
> **Joshua:** (sighs): "...deal."

**Trigger:** Fires when player drives Sarah's route and arrives within the interview window on time.

---

## Mission 12: The Score

**Objective:** Execute the heist (Loud or Quiet path).

**Dialogue (Quiet approach):**
> **Sarah** (in comms): "Guard change in ten. You're up."
> **Ben** (approaching): "How many fake realtors does it take?"
> **Alexandre:** "Just walk like you own the place."
> **Joshua:** "And the bag?"
> **Ben:** "That's the hard part."

**Dialogue (Loud approach):**
> **Joshua** (on radio): "Bridge approach. Three minutes."
> **Alexandre:** "Armoured truck moving. Now."
> **Sarah:** "Security net's down. Go."
> **Ben:** "Bags loaded. Move move move."

**Trigger:** Fires when player reaches job objective checkpoint (Coal Harbour presale or Lions Gate armoured truck).

---

## Mission 13: The Getaway

**Objective:** Reach the boat. Lose the police. Get to North Vancouver.

**Dialogue:**
> **Christine** (calling mid-getaway): "Joshua, honey, are you coming to dinner?"
> **Joshua:** "Not a great time, Mom."
> **Brian** (on boat radio): "Cops behind you. Cut east."
> **Joshua:** "Working on it!"
> **Christine** (still on phone): "I'm making your favourite..."
> **Joshua:** (click).

**Trigger:** Fires when player reaches Dad's boat at the Waterfront dock and cops reach 3-star heat.

---

## Mission 14: Waterfront

**Objective:** Meet Dmitri. Pay or don't. Finish it.

**Dialogue:**
> **Dmitri** (on the dock): "Forty grand was the deal."
> **Joshua:** "Here's forty-two. For the trouble."
> **Dmitri:** (counting): "You're funny. You're lucky, and you're funny."
> **Brian** (behind Joshua): "He gets it from his mother."
> **Christine:** "I made lasagna."
> **Sarah:** "Can we please just eat?"

**Trigger:** Fires when player enters Waterfront Station final checkpoint after escape.
