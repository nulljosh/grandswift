# Changelog

All the releases, newest first. Every version is tagged on GitHub with builds attached.

## 1.25 (2026-09-21)
SkyTrain on the real-streets map: raised track, the real downtown stations with name boards, a train running the line. Langley map. Roadmap rewritten with the full gap list against GTA 3 through 6. Proper repo docs.

## 1.24
Native apps only: Windows in C#, Linux in C, Mac and iOS in SwiftUI, Android in Java. Electron removed. CI builds every platform. Renamed to Vancouver Vice. Victoria on the real-streets map.

## 1.20 to 1.23
A realistic, diverse crowd. Each hero has their own body. GTA V style character switch. Clearer tutorial. Privacy policy. Real pistol model. No more soldier flash on load. Lighter frame rate. City, map and native tests from Codex merged.

## 1.10 to 1.19
Real downtown streets from OpenStreetMap. Street signs, traffic lights, streetlights. Walk into 2,609 real places. Rob a 7-Eleven. Traffic and cop chases on the real streets. Online play. A real person as the hero.

## 1.0 to 1.9
Weapons, car damage and explosions, radio, saves, story missions, live weather, three heroes with perks, neighbourhoods, reflective ocean and Kits Beach, web build and every platform, renamed from Grand Swift to Rainjack.

## 0.1 to 0.4
A top-down Vancouver in one Swift file, then first person, then third person, then guns and Victoria.
